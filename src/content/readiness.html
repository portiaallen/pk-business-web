Needs Attention

What PK found
Your books are set up and mostly current, but several months of transactions are sitting uncategorized and two accounts aren't matching your statements.
Why it matters
Uncategorized activity makes your numbers look worse — or better — than they really are, and anything built on top of those numbers inherits the error.
What to do next
Categorize the flagged months and reconcile the two accounts before anything is filed. Priority items are listed in order in your report.

Action Needed

What PK found
Income is arriving through more than one platform, and the supporting records for part of the year are spread across email, a phone camera roll and paper.
Why it matters
If records can't be located quickly, legitimate business activity can be hard to support — and you end up rebuilding the year from memory.
What to do next
Consolidate records into one organized structure and close the specific gaps PK lists for you by month.

Ready

What PK found
Your business information is organized well enough that a preparer could work from it once the items above are handled.
Why it matters
Going in organized means fewer surprises, fewer back-and-forth requests, and a calmer season.
What to do next
Keep the current routine, finish the priority items from the other two areas, and you're in good shape going into the season.

Questions, answered

Before you buy

Is this tax preparation?

No. This is a readiness assessment. It reviews where your books, records and business information stand going into tax season. It is not tax-return preparation or filing.

Will you fix my books during the assessment?

No. The assessment identifies where you currently stand. It does not include bookkeeping cleanup, correcting transactions, or completing reconciliations. If that work is needed, PK will tell you — clearly — and you choose what happens next.

What happens if my report says I need more work done?

Your report explains what's needed and why. If the recommended work is a service PK Business Services offers, you may claim the full $99 credit by expressing interest within 14 calendar days of final report delivery. After PK approval, redeem it against one qualifying invoice within six months of final report delivery. If it's something you can handle yourself, the report tells you how to approach it. And if the right help sits outside PK, your report names the type of professional to look for. Either way, the decision is yours.

What if my books are actually fine?

Then you'll have that in writing — which is worth knowing. "No action needed" is a legitimate result, and you still receive your written report and recommended next steps. PK will not invent work to sell you.

What information will PK need?

After purchase you'll complete an intake, then provide the business information PK requests through the secure PK process. You will never be asked to send Social Security numbers, ITINs, bank logins, tax returns or other sensitive taxpayer documents through this page.

What exactly does the $99 include?

A bookkeeping readiness review, reconciliation/status review, business income and expense organization review, recordkeeping/document readiness review, tax-season readiness review, missing-information and priority-item identification, your written PK Readiness Report, and recommended next steps.

Does the $99 apply toward other PK services?

Yes — toward one qualifying service that your own Readiness Report recommends, when you express interest within 14 calendar days of final report delivery and PK approves the claim. The approved credit must be redeemed against a valid qualifying invoice within six months of final report delivery. It cannot be divided or exceed the invoice amount. It isn't a universal credit: it can't be applied to an unrelated PK service, it isn't cash, it isn't separately refundable or transferable, and it can't be used with an outside provider.

What if the report says I should go somewhere else?

Then that's what it will say. If something falls outside what PK Business Services does, your report identifies the type of professional or service to consider and explains why — so you know what kind of help to look for. The $99 credit only applies to PK services, so it wouldn't carry over to an outside provider, but the recommendation is yours to keep and act on.

What happens after I purchase?

You complete your assessment intake, provide the requested information through the secure PK process, PK performs the assessment, and you receive your written PK Readiness Report with recommended next steps. Turnaround times are not published on this page — your report is delivered once PK completes the review of the information you provide.

My books are a mess. Is that going to be awkward?

No. PK is explicitly nonjudgmental. Disorganized books are one of the most normal things in small business, and "I don't know what I'm doing" is a completely acceptable starting point. The purpose of this assessment is clarity, not shame.
